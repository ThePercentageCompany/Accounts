# Business groups and country entities

Create a business group, then add each country's legal entity. Each entity has its own company identifier, country, accounting currency, tax registration and IANA business time zone. Its customers, employees, tasks, journals, financial periods, documents and access permissions remain separate.

The group overview shows authorized entities, employee/task counts and outstanding customer balances grouped by invoice currency. It does not convert currencies or produce consolidated financial statements. Open an entity for its accounting reports and operational modules.

The workspace is organized into Overview, Sales, People, Work, Accounting and System. The entity overview links to invoices, quotations, customers, employees, payroll, tasks, calendar, projects and financial reports.

Task dates and activity timestamps use the entity's business time zone, including daylight saving and half-hour offsets. Database reminders use the same zone. Tax registrations are stored as entity configuration; country-specific statutory tax returns and compliance rules are not automated.

Existing independent companies remain accessible in the group overview. New country entities are created with independent books; existing data is not merged or reassigned automatically.
